Pets are found in Lunchboxes and Pet Carriers.\n\nPets provide a bonus effect to the Dweller they're assigned to and increase that Dweller’s damage in combat. They can even tag along and help in the Wasteland.\n\nOnly one to three Pets are allowed in a room at a time, depending on the room's size.\n\nPets come in COMMON, RARE and LEGENDARY versions. The rarer the Pet, the greater its bonus effect, but even identical Pets might grant bonuses with different strengths.\n\nAssign a Pet like you would equip a Weapon or an Outfit.=你可以在避难所科技午餐盒或者从宠物笼获得宠物。\n\n宠物会为跟随的主人提供加成，在战斗中提升主人的伤害。它们甚至会跟主人去废土探索并提供帮助。\n\n一间房内最多能容纳一到三只宠物，取决于房间的大小。\n\n宠物也分为普通，稀有和传说三个稀有度。宠物越稀有，提供的加成也越多，但是同一稀有度的宠物根据不同能力获得的加成也不同。\n\n像服装武器和服装那样，为居民配上宠物吧。
